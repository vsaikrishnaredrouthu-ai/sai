Spintronic VLSI · EDA simulation & verification

Developed a novel low-power full-adder architecture built on spintronic concepts, targeting ultra-low-power, high-speed arithmetic and a significant reduction in power-delay product (PDP).

  • Developed a low-power full-adder architecture using spintronic concepts.
  • Improved reliability by introducing a post-processing unit.
  • Achieved reduced power consumption and delay for high-speed digital applications.
